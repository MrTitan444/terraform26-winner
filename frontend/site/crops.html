Your hands are the ruler.

This guide never asks for numbers from a machine. Every size is something you can measure with your body.

  • A finger-lengthFrom the tip of your pointing finger to where it meets your hand.
  • A hand’s spanSpread your hand wide, thumb tip to little-finger tip. About 20 cm.
  • A hand’s depthAs deep as your hand is long. Used for digging.
  • Knee-highFrom the ground to your knee. About 45–50 cm.
  • Waist-highFrom the ground to your waist. Good for climbing sticks.
  • A paceOne big step. About 1 metre.
  • A handful / two cupped palmsWhat one hand holds / what two hands cupped together hold.
  • A bucketfulA normal bucket, about 10 litres.
  • A wheelbarrow-loadA full wheelbarrow.
  • A sunOne day. “Ten suns” means ten days.
  • A moonOne moon cycle, about 4 weeks.
  • 100 breaths / 500 pacesUsed for short waits and walking times.

In-field hand checks.

No lab needed. Test your patch right now with your eyes, hands, a stick, and rainwater.

  • Dig a hand’s depthDig a hole a hand’s depth deep. Look underneath: is it clean soil, rubble, smelly/stained earth, or rock hard?
  • Smell after rainCrouch down after rain. Earthy is healthy; sour, rotten egg, or fuel smell warns of poison.
  • Water drainageWatch rain puddles. Fast soaking is good; sitting for days means waterlogged roots.
  • Squeeze testSqueeze damp soil. Breadcrumbs = good loam; ribbon longer than 2 thumb joints = heavy clay; runs loose = sand.
  • Squish testSqueeze wet handful hard. Springy like a sponge = living soil; rock hard = exhausted; slimy = suffocating.
  • Stick push testPush a thumb-thick green stick straight down with body weight. Less than a hand-width = ground is packed too hard for roots.
  • Rainwater jar testShake a handful of soil in a clear jar of rainwater. Wait 100 breaths. Check for oily rainbow sheen or fuel smells.
  • Sprout testPlant bean seeds in site cup vs clean soil cup side-by-side. Curled or blackened leaf tips warn of toxins.
Step 06 · Choose a crop

Ten safe crops
to start with.

All ten grow above the ground in a clean bed or container. Each one opens its own pages: how long it takes, how to plant, care, and harvest.

Safety rule. Never grow carrots, potatoes, radishes or leafy greens straight in suspect ground. Skip sunflowers there too: they pull poison up into the plant. Grow only in your clean raised bed or containers.
