Your hands are the ruler.

This guide never asks for numbers from a machine. Every size is something you can measure with your body.

  • A finger-lengthFrom the tip of your pointing finger to where it meets your hand.
  • A hand’s spanSpread your hand wide, thumb tip to little-finger tip. About 20 cm.
  • A hand’s depthAs deep as your hand is long. Used for digging.
  • Knee-highFrom the ground to your knee. About 45–50 cm.
  • Waist-highFrom the ground to your waist. Good for climbing sticks.
  • A paceOne big step. About 1 metre.
  • A handful / two cupped palmsWhat one hand holds / what two hands cupped together hold.
  • A bucketfulA normal bucket, about 10 litres.
  • A wheelbarrow-loadA full wheelbarrow.
  • A sunOne day. “Ten suns” means ten days.
  • A moonOne moon cycle, about 4 weeks.
  • 100 breaths / 500 pacesUsed for short waits and walking times.
Step 04 · Step-by-step fix

Don’t fix the ground.
Grow above it.

Poisoned or packed ground is slow to heal. So we cover it, build up from it, and fill the bed with clean material. Tick each step off as you go.

Picture it

A knee-high bed, from the bottom up

The growing mix

For every pace of bed length

2buckets of rotted forest leaves
1bucket of crushed charcoal or cold campfire ash
2buckets of compost

Use clean material from outside the site. Never mix in soil from the damaged ground.
