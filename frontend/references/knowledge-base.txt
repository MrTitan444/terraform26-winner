# TERRAFORM Knowledge Base: Architecture & Implementation Blueprint

## 1. System Vision & Ground Rules

**Project Premise:**  
TERRAFORM is an offline survivor intelligence system built to guide an untrained survivor from bare, hazardous, post-collapse terrain to a sustainable harvest without access to laboratories, digital instruments, or commercial agricultural chemicals.

### Non-Negotiable Survivor Ground Rules:
1. **Zero Scientific Readings:** The system **never** requests, quotes, or calculates numbers for pH, NPK ppm, electrical conductivity (EC), moisture percentages, or microscopic spore counts.
2. **Everyday Human Measurements Only:**
   - *Volumes:* "a handful", "two cupped palms", "a bucketful" (~10L), "a wheelbarrow-load".
   - *Lengths & Depths:* "a finger-length", "a hand's span" (~20 cm), "a hand's depth", "knee-high" (~45-50 cm), "waist-high", "a pace" (~1 meter).
   - *Time:* "a moon cycle" (~4 weeks), "morning vs evening sun", "weeks / seasons".
3. **Safety-First Precautionary Principle:**
   - Unidentified stains, rust, chemical odors, or industrial rubble = **assumed hazardous (heavy metals, petrochemicals, toxic runoff)**.
   - Ground crops (potatoes, carrots, radishes, leafy greens) are strictly forbidden directly in suspect soil.
   - Default remedy for compromised terrain is **physical isolation** (rubble/canvas/cardboard barrier) + **knee-high raised beds or deep containers** built with uncontaminated external organic matter.
4. **Faithful Intake:**
   - Restates observed facts (smell, surface condition, past use, debris, stains) without hallucinating details.
   - Explicitly flags and itemizes what has **not yet been checked**.

## 3. The 10 Core Functional Modules

### Module 1: Soil Diagnosis & Qualitative Nutrient Assessment
- **Faithful Restatement Engine:** Echoes inputs verbatim:
  - *Observed:* Stains (black, iridescent, chemical), smells (sour, rotten egg, metallic, petrichor, inert), debris (concrete, rebar, glass, slag), existing vegetation (thistles, clumpy weeds, bare spots).
  - *Unchecked Checklist:* Marks items the survivor hasn't inspected yet (e.g., subsurface texture at a hand's depth, smell after rain, standing water drainage speed).
- **Sensory Nutrient Tests (No instruments):**
  - *Nitrogen clue:* Pale yellowing stunted wild weeds = poor; lush, dark-green weeds (nettles, dock) = high organic nitrogen.
  - *Drainage & Texture:* The "Squeeze & Ribbon Test" using palms (crumbles like breadcrumbs = loam; forms sticky ribbon longer than two thumb-joints = heavy clay; runs through fingers = sterile sand).
  - *Organic Matter (Squish Test):* Squeeze a wet handful; sponge-like bounce back = humus rich; rock hard or slime = degraded.

### Module 2: Soil Archetype Identification & Risk Assessment
- **Archetype Categorization:**
  1. *Compacted Urban Fill* (heavy foot/vehicle compaction, impervious, low air).
  2. *Industrial Rubble & Slag* (crushed concrete, brick, mortar, high alkalinity, potential lead/asbestos).
  3. *Disturbed Soil of Unknown Safety* (stains, fuel/chemical smells, scrap metal, irregular clumping weeds).
  4. *Degraded Post-Agricultural Silt/Clay* (crusted, eroded, nutrient-exhausted).
  5. *Sandy / Gravelly Washout* (fast leaching, zero water retention).
- **Simple No-Equipment Risk Checks:**
  - *The Jar Sheen & Settle Test:* Shake a handful of soil in a clear jar of rainwater. Let settle for 100 breaths. Check surface for rainbow/oil sheen or petrochemical odor.
  - *The Stick Probe Penetration Test:* Push a thumb-thick green wooden stick straight down with full body weight. Measure penetration against hand-widths (less than one hand-width = root-suffocating compaction).
  - *The Fast-Sprout Bioassay (Canary Check):* Compare wild seeds or fast-sprouting beans in a cup of site soil vs. a cup of boiled/leaf-mold clean soil.
- **Explicit Negative Scope (What checks CANNOT prove):**
  - *Warning Prompt:* "These tests cannot detect invisible heavy metals (lead, arsenic, cadmium) or odorless persistent pesticides. Never plant direct root crops in unverified disturbed soil."

### Module 3: Crop Selection Engine (Offline JSON Catalog)
- **Crop Profile Database Schema:**
  - Name, Category (Heavy Feeder, Light Feeder, Soil Builder/Legume, Scavenger).
  - Safety tier (Surface fruiting vs subterranean root vs bioaccumulator to avoid like sunflowers in toxic soil).
  - Growth duration (fast 3-4 weeks, medium 8-12 weeks, long 16+ weeks).
  - Survivor value (caloric density, vitamin content, medicinal, seed-saving ease).
  - Tolerances (poor drainage, drought, alkaline rubble lime, shaded collapse walls).
- **Matching Algorithm:**
  - Matches site constraints (sunlight paces, bed depth achieved, water availability) against safe survivor staples (e.g., Bush Beans, Squash/Pumpkins, Chard/Kale in raised beds, Dwarf Corn, Sweet Potatoes in clean mounds).

### Module 4: Immediate Soil Remedies (Hand-Measured Protocols)
- **Physical Remediation Recipes:**
  - *The Barrier Method:* Pick clean large debris with hands. Lay down 3 layers of wet cardboard, burlap, or woven canvas over contaminated ground.
  - *Knee-High Raised Bed Construction:* Stack salvaged stones/bricks knee-high (~45 cm).
  - *The Hugelkultur Core:* Bottom half-filled with rotting logs, thumb-thick branches, and dry leaves.
  - *Top Growing Layer:* 2 bucketfuls of forest leaf-mold + 1 bucketful of crushed charcoal/ash from campfires + 2 bucketfuls of composted plant matter per pace of bed length.
  - *Container Alternative:* Deep salvaged barrels, buckets, or tires lined with canvas.

### Module 5: Readiness Verification Check
- **Pre-Planting Gateway Protocol:**
  - [ ] *The Puddle Drain Test:* Dig a hole one hand-depth deep. Fill with a bucket of water. Must drain within the time it takes to walk 500 paces.
  - [ ] *The Heat & Settle Check:* Bed has rested for 5 to 7 sun-cycles; does not feel hot to the back of the hand (ensuring unfinished compost won't burn seedling roots).
  - [ ] *The Sprout Greenlight:* Test seeds sprouted without curled, blackened leaf tips.

### Module 6: Crop Timeline & Survival Buffer Calculation
- **Realistic Timeline Range:**
  - Formula: $\text{Total Timeline} = \text{Clearing \& Debris Buffer (1-2 wks)} + \text{Bed Construction \& Settling Buffer (1-2 wks)} + \text{Crop Seed-to-Harvest Time (Wks)}$.
- **Visual Milestone Tracker:**
  - Week 0-2: Land lockdown, barrier laying, bed building, bioassay test.
  - Week 3-4: Direct sowing / seedling emergence (cotyledon stage).
  - Week 5-8: Rapid vegetative growth, leaf harvesting (for continuous greens).
  - Harvest Window: Primary caloric harvest + seed preservation.

### Module 7: Crop Failure & Setback Troubleshooting
- **Symptom-Action Matrices:**
  - Stalled growth after transplanting $\rightarrow$ Root shock or cold ground $\rightarrow$ Dome with cut plastic jugs/dry grass mulch ring.
  - Sudden wilting despite damp soil $\rightarrow$ Root rot / zero drainage $\rightarrow$ Aerate bed edge with stick probe, pull mulch back from stem base.
  - Spindly, leggy, falling seedlings $\rightarrow$ Starved for light behind collapsed walls $\rightarrow$ White sheet / foil reflector placement.

### Module 8: Pest & Disease Diagnosis with DIY Remedies
- **Visual Symptom Diagnostic:**
  - Holes chewed in leaves $\rightarrow$ Beetles / caterpillars $\rightarrow$ Morning hand-picking + dust with dry wood ash.
  - Fine webbing under leaves / bronzed speckling $\rightarrow$ Mites $\rightarrow$ Garlic & wild onion crushed in a water jar, sprayed/flicked with leafy branch.
  - White powdery film on leaves $\rightarrow$ Powdery mildew $\rightarrow$ Dilute milk wash (1 cup milk or whey to a bucket of water) or baking soda wash.
  - Yellow mosaic blotches $\rightarrow$ Viral infection $\rightarrow$ Immediate cull and burn outside garden perimeter (never compost infected plants).

### Module 9: Crop Rotation Cycles & Pioneer Cover Crops
- **The 4-Stage Survivor Rotation Cycle:**
  1. *Stage 1: Heavy Feeders* (Corn, Squash, Tomatoes, Cabbage) in newly rich beds.
  2. *Stage 2: Light Feeders* (Greens, Herbs, Peppers) utilizing remaining moderate nutrients.
  3. *Stage 3: Soil Builders / Legumes* (Beans, Peas, Clovers) pulling nitrogen from the air into root nodules.
  4. *Stage 4: Deep Scavengers or Cover Crop Rest* (Mustard, Buckwheat, Rye grass) chopped and dropped in place as green manure.

### Module 10: Field Clearing & Post-Harvest Re-enrichment
- **Visual Deficiency Key:**
  - Lower leaves yellowing first $\rightarrow$ Nitrogen starvation $\rightarrow$ Diluted nettle tea or aged weed tea (steeped 10 suns in a water bucket).
  - Purple/red tinting on stems and undersides $\rightarrow$ Phosphorus starvation $\rightarrow$ Crushed bone meal from cookout bones charred in campfire.
  - Scorched/curled leaf margins $\rightarrow$ Potassium starvation $\rightarrow$ Light dusting of cold hardwood ashes soaked in water (ash tea).
  - Blossom-end rot / dying growing tips $\rightarrow$ Calcium lock $\rightarrow$ Crushed eggshells / snail shells steeped in vinegar or rainwater.

## 5. Laptop 1 & Laptop 2 Collaboration Contract

To ensure seamless integration between the Knowledge Base team and the Interface team:
1. **Standardized API Schema:** All responses will return clean JSON with standard fields: `status`, `summary`, `survivor_instructions`, `hand_measurements`, `safety_warnings`, and `checklist`.
2. **Mock Server:** Provide mock JSON responses within the first 30 minutes so Laptop 2 can build UI components immediately without waiting for full reasoning completion.
3. **Zero Dependency Footprint:** Everything runs offline without internet access (local Python virtual environment or Node.js runtime).
